# Story

A music video made with this skill is a short film. The song is the clock and the emotional key, not a list of nouns to illustrate.

## Three concepts before any drawing

Each concept needs:

- a person who wants something
- a second force (another person, a machine, a deadline)
- a visible object that changes meaning (the motif)
- a different picture for each repeated chorus
- an ending image that rhymes with the opening image

Reject a concept that is only weather, stars, clocks, or floating dust.

## Scoring

Score 1–5: lyric fit, completeness, emotion, originality, code feasibility, long-form watchability. Pick the highest total that you can actually animate with two reusable sets and two character rigs.

## Shot length

Cut on musical phrases and on changes of intention. A verse can hold a 8–12s shot if the camera and the body keep moving. A chorus hit can be 2–4s. Do not give every line the same duration.

## Chorus repeats

Same motif, different state. Example: first chorus the hands almost meet; second chorus the object opens; final chorus the object is lost or given. Never replay a shot with the same camera and the same pose.

## Motif

Choose one object that can be held, broken, and returned. Track it in the shot list so a viewer who ignores the lyric can still follow it.
