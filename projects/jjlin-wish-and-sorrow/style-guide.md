# Style guide — 《最后的果园》 / The Last Orchard

A 3:52.6 animated interpretation of 林俊杰《愿与愁》. 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps.
The film is an **ink-silhouette short**: black figures against painted weather, with three
colours allowed to carry meaning — seed-green, scarf-amber, signal-red.

## Thesis

Love is not the refusal of dust; it is the act of remembering together that turns dust into rain.

## Why silhouettes

The production tested a fully painted face rig and rejected it: at 1080p the faces read as
illustration, not film. The silhouette is a deliberate abstraction. It protects the film from
uncanny detail and lets pose, scale, weather and light do the acting. Close-ups are reserved for
what the silhouette cannot carry: an eye, a hand, a seed, a paper crane.

## Palette

| role | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| bone paper | `#e8dcc6` | base, title, transitions |
| ash grey | `#8d8579` | dust, distance |
| umber | `#4a3527` | earth, wood |
| deep teal | `#1d3434` | interior shadow, storm |
| moss | `#5c7250` | orchard life |
| seed green | `#b9d98a` | the seed, the tree, hope |
| lamp amber | `#f0c477` | warmth, the lamp |
| scarf amber | `#a86137` | Mu's one colour |
| signal red | `#d5482f` | the ark's countdown |
| rain silver | `#a9c5c8` | release, the coda |
| silhouette | `#14110f` (Mu), `#101416` (Chen) | bodies |

Colour script: ash/grey → amber/moss (shelter) → indigo/black (storm) → jade/gold/silver (rebirth)
→ pale green/bone (coda). The only saturated colours in Act I are the seed and the scarf; the
world earns colour back at the climax.

## Light

One key, one fill, one rim. Interiors: a low lamp (amber) against a cold dome (teal); faces are
planes, not portraits. Exteriors: a pale sun through ash; silhouettes are backlit, with a thin
warm rim. Storm: lightning as a 2–4 frame white accent, not a strobe. The final valley: high key,
soft, almost over-exposed, to feel like memory rather than victory.

## Line and texture

- Charcoal/ink contours with variable pressure; 12 fps boil on the figure, 24 fps camera.
- Paper grain, watercolour bloom, dry-brush edges; no clean vector gradients as final surfaces.
- Pigment pass over every frame: wash (multiply), dry-brush (screen), warm/cool grade, vignette.
- Keep edges soft at distance; let the foreground go dark and out of focus.

## 9:16 composition

- Vertical architecture: dome ribs, ladder, tree, rain, doorway.
- Faces upper third; hands/props lower third; the middle is relation/negative space.
- Alternate EWS (world), MS (relationship), macro (choice). No run of identical medium shots.
- Foreground occlusion in most shots: pots, glass, branches, shoulders, shards.
- The camera has a motive: push = realisation, pull = release, crane = fate, handheld = danger,
  orbit = relation. Static is allowed when the subject moves.

## Characters

**Mu** — 22, last gardener. Short black bob, amber scarf, oversized olive work coat, patched
trousers, boots. Silhouette: narrow vertical, coat hem, scarf tail. Want: the boy to return.
Need: to share the future rather than guard it. Arc: guardian → host → co-creator.

**Chen** — 24, sky scavenger. Shaved sides, slate flight suit, harness with red signal, worn coat,
cracked mask at the neck, scar through the right brow. Silhouette: taller, angular, one shoulder
higher. Want: save the ark. Need: remember where he came from. Arc: thief → guest → partner.

**The seed/tree** — a green coal wrapped in roots; opens only for two people. Stage 0 sprout →
young tree → giant tree → rain of seeds. The tree is drawn as a recursive ink structure, not a
lollipop: trunk, branching, fine leaf clusters, roots.

## Props and motifs

Paper crane (his and hers; tied together at the end), glass vial of soil/ashes, dropper, tally
wall, cracked mask, wrist signal, lantern, launch pod, glass shards, the pool. Every motif returns
changed: ash → rain; seed → tree; separated cranes → one branch; a guarded door → an open valley.

## Transitions

Hard cuts on musical events; shape matches (seed/eye/drop); dust wipe; glass shatter; whiteout
into the coda; final fade to paper. No random cross-dissolves, no film-burn overlays.

## Typography

Only the end title: 愿与愁 (Noto Serif CJK SC) and THE LAST ORCHARD, in ink on the pool image.
No lyric text is burned into the film. Captions are a separate SRT.

## Anti-template rules

- No lyric noun illustrated literally.
- No repeated chorus composition.
- No floating particles without a narrative cause.
- No zoom on a still frame presented as animation.
- No identical shot twice; repeated imagery must change state.
- No empty black frame, no duplicate frame to hide a render failure.
