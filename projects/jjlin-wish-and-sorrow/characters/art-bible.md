# Character and art bible

## Mu (暮)

- **Age / build:** 22, 1.62 m, wiry, slightly hunched from greenhouse work.
- **Silhouette:** short bob with a soft jaw-length mass; long work coat to mid-thigh; scarf tail
  breaks the vertical; boots planted wide when she works.
- **Wardrobe:** olive canvas coat patched at the elbows, bone thermal shirt, dark trousers,
  fingerless gloves, amber scarf (her mother's), glass vial on a cord.
- **Face (for the eye/hand close-ups):** warm ochre skin, dark amber eyes, straight brows, a small
  burn scar on the left forearm, chapped lips.
- **Posture:** holds her hands close; looks down when tired; squares her shoulders before a choice.
- **Arc:** guarding a memory → allowing a shared future.

## Chen (尘)

- **Age / build:** 24, 1.78 m, lean, angular; one shoulder higher from the harness.
- **Silhouette:** taller, narrower hips, short crop with shaved sides, flight suit + stolen coat,
  mask hanging at the neck, red signal on the wrist.
- **Wardrobe:** slate flight suit, rust harness, worn umber coat, cracked oxygen mask, boots.
- **Face:** warm umber skin, dark eyes, scar through the right brow, cracked lips.
- **Posture:** enters low and guarded; opens his hands before he speaks; reaches back for her.
- **Arc:** taking what he needs → choosing what he loves.

## The seed / tree

A green coal with a seam, wrapped in white roots. It glows from within, brighter when two hands
hold it. Stage 0: two leaves. Stage 1: a young tree, 6–8 m. Stage 2: a giant tree, canopy filling
the top third, roots breaking stone. Stage 3: a rain of seeds. Draw with recursive ink branches
and fine leaf clusters; avoid a single blob canopy.

## The orchard

A patched geodesic glass dome on a cliff: 11 ribs, three rings, a central raised bed, shelves of
empty pots, a hand-crank ladder, a worktable, a hanging lamp, a tally wall, a door to the storm.
The dome is protection and isolation; it must break for the film to end.

## The ark

A whale-like generation ship: long spine, rib lights, hanging modules, no aggression in its shape.
It is a dying home, not a villain. It descends at the end.

## Continuity rules

- Mu's scarf is always on the left shoulder, tail falling to her right hip.
- Chen's mask is at his neck except when removed; the red signal is on his left wrist.
- The seed is never duplicated; when it opens, the seam stays visible.
- The scar and the paper crane are the recognition clues; keep them readable in close-up.
- Wardrobe changes only at the coda (older selves), and is shown through grey hair/posture,
  not a new costume.
