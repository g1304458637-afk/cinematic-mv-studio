# Three original film concepts

Song: 林俊杰《愿与愁》 (Dust and Ashes) · 3:52.6 supplied master
Working title of the selected film: **《最后的果园》 / The Last Orchard**

The brief: a self-contained animated short in 9:16, carried by image and music, not a lyric
illustration. The song's themes — wish and sorrow, dust and rain, memory and forgetting,
meeting and parting, love across time and space — are the emotional materials, not a shot list.

---

## Concept A — The Last Orchard (selected)

**Logline.** In a world buried by ash, a young gardener guarding the last living seed shelters a
sky-dweller who came to steal it. The seed will only open for two people who choose to stay.

**Story.** Mu has tended a glass orchard on a cliff for six years, waiting for someone who was
taken to the ark above. A pod falls; the intruder is Chen, the boy who once gave her a paper
crane — but the ark has conditioned him to forget the surface. He needs the last seed for his
dying people; she needs it to keep her mother's memory alive. They fight, then repair the dome
together during a storm. The seed opens at their touch. The ark's countdown forces him to choose:
he takes the pod and leaves. Too late he finds it empty; she kept the seed. He turns back as the
storm destroys the dome. They shelter the seed with their bodies; it becomes a tree, the ash turns
to rain, and the ark comes home. In the coda he remembers her name.

**Why it fits the song.** The lyric's movement from dust → waiting → rain → "only for the moment
of reunion" is the film's literal transformation. The repeated chorus is not repeated imagery:
chorus 1 is recognition in a closed warm room; chorus 2 is the seed opening and joy; the final
chorus is the storm, the tree and the rain. "Wish" and "sorrow" are two characters, two losses,
and one shared choice.

| Criterion | Score /5 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Lyric / music fit | 5 | dust, rain, waiting, reunion, "ten fingers clasped" all have story verbs |
| Story completeness | 5 | clear want, obstacle, midpoint reversal, crisis, climax, coda |
| Emotional impact | 5 | recognition, betrayal-that-isn't, mutual rescue, reunion |
| Visual originality | 4 | familiar post-apocalyptic greenhouse, made specific by the memory-seed and paper cranes |
| Code feasibility | 4 | 3 main sets + 1 vehicle; two human rigs; particles and tree growth are procedural |
| Long-form watchability | 5 | four visual chapters, changing palette/space, no repeated chorus composition |

**Risks.** The "last plant in a dead world" image is familiar; the film must earn its specificity
through the memory-seed, the two paper cranes, the ark's conditioning, and the final reunion.
Avoid *Interstellar* imagery (bookshelf, ice planet, docking spin) and the official MV's imagery.

## Concept B — The Rain Archive

**Logline.** In a drowned city, a librarian stores the last rains in glass bottles. A boy who
drowned returns with the tide and asks her to break every bottle so the city can begin again.

**Story.** Rain is extinct; the Archive keeps bottled storms as memories. The librarian, Lin, has
spent her life preserving them. A boy, Ash, arrives dry, though everyone else is waterlogged; he
is the city's last wish, not a person. To save the city from eternal stagnation, Lin must smash
the archive; each bottle she breaks erases a memory of Ash. She chooses the future over the
preserved past, and the city floods green.

**Evaluation.** 4 lyric fit (rain, memory, letting go), 4 story completeness (repetitive middle),
4 emotional impact, 5 visual originality (bottled weather, underwater rooms), 3 code feasibility
(water caustics and glass refraction at 1080×1920 are expensive and risky), 4 watchability.
**Total 24/30.** Not selected: the middle is a sequence of bottle-breaks, and the character
relationship is more allegorical than dramatic.

---

## Concept C — The Clockmaker's Apprentice

**Logline.** A girl repairs the town clock that controls time. A boy from a future where the
clock stopped comes back to steal one gear; every repair erases a memory of him.

**Story.** The clock is a mechanical god; the town lives the same perfect day. The apprentice,
Nian, discovers the boy, Shi, who is a repairman from the stopped future. Each time she fixes a
gear, the town advances a day but she forgets Shi. In the end she leaves the clock broken and
walks into an unknown future with him.

**Evaluation.** 4 lyric fit (time, permanence, "crush the hourglass"), 5 story completeness,
3 emotional impact (the memory-loss mechanic is clever but risks coldness), 3 visual originality
(clock towers and gears are well-worn), 5 code feasibility (rigid-body mechanisms are easy),
4 watchability. **Total 24/30.** Not selected: the central mechanic is more interesting than the
people, and the "clock/time" imagery is the most template-prone of the three.

---

## Selection

**A — The Last Orchard**, 28/30. It gives every musical section a visible change of state and
keeps the two leads in the same frame for the emotional climax. The production is designed around
three reusable sets (orchard interior, cliff exterior, storm/tree aftermath), one pod interior,
and two character rigs — feasible at feature quality in the available render budget.

Working title: **《最后的果园》** · English: **The Last Orchard**
Parent work: 《愿与愁》 (Dust and Ashes) — an original, non-official animated interpretation.
